1. Abstract

NOIDCAT is a 500,000 supply native token created at Parano1d mainnet block 279,370, the activation height of the v2.1 native asset rules. It launches through the chain's built-in launch curve, with no presale, no allowlist, no private round, and no mint authority.

The interesting claim is not the cat. It is that a meme token here inherits properties that normally require a contract, an audit and trust in a team: fixed supply enforced by consensus, holder backing redeemable by burn, a public curve with published formulas, and a graduation path into a permanent pool. None of it is a smart contract. It is protocol behaviour, identical for every token launched the same way.

2. The chain

Parano1d is a proof-native, post-quantum proof-of-work L1. Blocks target 30 seconds. Addresses are bech32m with an o1 prefix. NOID has six decimal places and is denominated in micronoid (1 NOID = 1,000,000 μNOID); every amount in the RPC surface, this document and the tooling is an integer count of micronoid.

v2.1 introduced native assets: tokens, collections and markets executed by consensus rather than by deployed bytecode. Native operations open at height 279,370. Below that height every preview fails with a deterministic error, which is why nothing can be pre-signed or bundled early.

3. The launch primitive

Creation is a single call, create_token, taking a supply, a metadata hash and an optional initial buy. Creation and the initial buy confirm atomically in one transaction — either both land or neither does. There is no window between the token existing and the first purchase in which another party can act.

  • Minimum supply 100,000 whole tokens. Maximum 10,248,191,152.
  • Metadata is a 32-byte hash committed on chain. The document behind it is immutable for the life of the token, which is why the image and description are finalised before creation rather than after.
  • No mint authority. The supply is fixed at creation. There is no later issuance and no creator withdrawal of holder backing.
  • Initial buy executes at the same curve price as any public buy, with no discount and no reserved allocation.

4. Curve mathematics

60% of supply (C = 300,000 tokens) is sold on a linear bonding curve. The remaining 40% never touches the curve and becomes permanent pool liquidity at graduation.

Price is linear in tokens sold. It opens at one third of a thousandth of a NOID and reaches graduation price exactly when the curve allocation is exhausted:

p(q) = p₀ + k·q,   p₀ = 1/300 NOID,  p(C) = 0.01 NOID,  k = (p₁ − p₀) / C

Buying q tokens along the curve costs the integral of price over that span, the area under the line:

cash(q) = p₀·q + k·q²/2

A sell of the same q tokens returns cash(q₀) − cash(q₀ − q) — the exact inverse. Two consequences follow, and both matter:

  • Curve trades carry no market fee. The 0.3% fee exists only on the DEX after graduation. A round trip on the curve costs only the network fee.
  • Curve trades are path-independent in price terms. Buying and immediately selling back returns the curve to its prior state, so two-sided volume does not decay the price by itself.

5. Backing and redemption

Every whole token carries a backing claim of 0.001 NOID, deposited by the creator at a total of 500 NOID for 500,000 tokens. Backing is attached to the token, not to an address: it travels with transfers and is claimable by whoever holds it.

burn_token destroys tokens and pays out their share of backing in the same transaction. Redemption is therefore always available and always proportional, and the creator cannot front-run it or withdraw the pool.

Backing is a floor, not a guarantee of value. 500 NOID is roughly what the token costs to exhaust at the very bottom of the curve, so backing sits below the curve's opening price — it protects the downside of a total collapse, not a normal drawdown.

6. Graduation and the pool

When the curve allocation is exhausted — 2,000 NOID of curve cash for this supply — the asset graduates automatically. The remaining 200,000 tokens and the curve proceeds form a permanent NOID pool. Balances and identities are unchanged; the venue changes from curve to DEX.

  • DEX trades charge 0.3% of input, rounded to a base unit, plus the network fee.
  • Automatic price tolerance is 10%. A caller can set their own minimum output; unspent inputs are released and an unfilled policy pays no fee.
  • Several trades can execute in one block, each against the state the previous one left. A fill always receives at least its authorised minimum.
  • Graduation is not a listing and not an announcement. It is a state transition.

8. Trading, lock and exit

Everything the dev wallet does after confirmation is scripted and published, not decided at two in the morning.

The lock

The team bag cannot be sold below twice its own average entry price. Below that the bag is inert — the price can fall and the only seller stays out of the way. Selling begins at 2× cost, in 8% clips at least 120 blocks apart, and 25% is held through graduation into the pool. Parano1d v2.1 has no vesting primitive, so this is script behaviour rather than a contract, and any break in it is visible on a public address.

Demand following

When a block moves the price more than 150 basis points without a team buy in it, the dev wallet buys in the following block. The accumulation is not allowed to be outrun by real flow: if buyers arrive, the bag fills faster and the price is defended by our own participation rather than by hope.

Exit

The dev wallet sells on a ladder at 2×, 3×, 5× and 8× its own average entry, 8% of the bag per rung, at least 120 blocks apart, never below average entry, and keeps at least 25% through graduation. Full detail in the disclosure.

9. Risk and threat model

What can go wrong, stated plainly:

  • Liquidity is thin until graduation. 2,000 NOID is the entire curve. A 20 NOID sell moves price several percent. Position sizes matter more here than on a deep venue.
  • The team trades early. Early candles are mostly the dev wallet's, so read the first hours as a starting price rather than a crowd.
  • Graduation is not permanent liquidity magic. The pool can be drained by sellers; the 40% becomes the only depth.
  • The lock is not a contract. Parano1d v2.1 has no vesting primitive. The 2× floor is script behaviour; a compromised key or a changed rule breaks it and the recourse is that the address is public.
  • Backing is small. 500 NOID, about a hundred and forty dollars at current prices, covers a collapse and nothing else.
  • Chain risk. Native assets are five weeks old at launch. Consensus bugs, RPC quirks and wallet tooling are all live risks in a way that a decade-old chain's are not.
  • Meme risk. There is no cash flow, no product and no claim on anything. If nobody laughs, the price is the backing and no more.

10. How to verify all of this

Nothing in this document needs to be taken on faith:

  • The metadata hash is committed on chain — read the token record and hash the JSON behind this site yourself.
  • Backing, supply and the absence of a mint are consensus rules of create_token, not policy.
  • Curve cash follows the formula above; the wallet preview exposes the exact debit, fee and quoted output before any signature.
  • The dev address is published in the disclosure and every trade from it is a public transaction on a public chain.
