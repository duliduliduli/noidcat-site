1. The dev wallet

One wallet creates the token, holds the team bag and pays for the build: hosting, art, liquidity and the launch itself. It is the only wallet the team trades from, and it is published here so the lock can be checked rather than trusted.

Locked until 2× the launch price

No token the team holds can reach the curve below twice the average price it was bought at. The bag sits still while the chart finds its level, and the first sale is only possible once the price has doubled. From there it unlocks in steps — never all at once — and a quarter of it stays locked through graduation.

  • Nothing sells below 2× average cost. Nobody buying the chart is bidding against the team's own exit.
  • Maximum 8% of the bag per unlock, at six rungs out to 150×, at least 120 blocks apart.
  • 25% stays locked through graduation, into the permanent DEX pool.

2. Buy plan

The token is created at the first block where v2.1 native operations are live, block 279,370, with a creation buy in the same transaction. One wallet, one transaction, one address in the block.

  • Creation buy: 25,000 tokens (5% of supply), paid at curve price, about 90 NOID. This sets the opening price and starts the chart.
  • Team target: 100,000 tokens (20% of supply), reached by accumulation on the public curve over the first hours — not in one block.
  • Hard cap: 22%. Past that, the dev wallet stops buying regardless of price.
  • Bought in clips, not one sweep. The dev wallet takes small slices across the first hours instead of a single order parked on the curve.

Early hours on a chart are mostly the people who built it, and this launch is no exception. What is fixed in advance is the ceiling and the exit: the team never holds more than 22%, and never sells below twice its own cost.

3. Sell ladder and lock

The dev wallet is the only seller, and it sells on a fixed ladder measured against its own average entry price. Nothing is sold below that average, and nothing at all below twice it.

  • Spacing: at least 120 blocks between rungs, so a single candle cannot trigger two sales.
  • Floor: the dev wallet keeps at least 25% of the bag through graduation and into the DEX pool.
  • Path: every sale goes through the public curve or pool at the same price everyone else sees. No off-market transfers to buyers.
  • Sells are not hidden. The bag sits in a public address; anyone can watch it move.

4. What this is not

  • Not enforced by a vesting contract. Parano1d v2.1 has none. The lock is script behaviour, published before launch — break it and it shows.
  • Not a presale and not an allowlist. There is no allocation to buy and nobody gets in early.
  • Not a treasury or a roadmap. The dev allocation pays for hosting, art and liquidity, not a team salary.
  • Not a claim about price. At launch prices the whole team bag is worth about a hundred dollars. Everything above that is whatever buyers decide.

Backing and supply. Supply is fixed at 500,000 with no mint. Backing is 0.001 NOID per token — 500 NOID in total — deposited at creation, attached permanently to the supply, redeemable by any holder who burns tokens. It is a floor, not a vault. The dev wallet can never withdraw it.
