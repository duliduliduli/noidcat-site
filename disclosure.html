02 / Buy plan

Creation buy14,000tokens, paid at curve price (~48.8 NOID)
Follow-on buys50 NOIDspread over 10–15 buys, not one block
Spacing5–7 blocks1–4 NOID each, randomized
Cap45 NOIDhard stop on the buy wallet

There is no creation-block bundle: the token is created once, on its own, at the first block native tokens are usable. Follow-on buys start after creation confirms and are paced so the price path is visible rather than a single jump.

Buy wallet holds 50 NOID. Leftover NOID after the plan stays in that wallet and is visible on-chain.

03 / Sell rule

  1. 1

    First sell: 10% of the team bag

    Only once the price holds at or above 0.006 NOID per token for 40 consecutive blocks. No selling into a flat or falling curve.

  2. 2

    Then 10% every 120 blocks

    Same rule repeats while the price holds, up to three more steps.

  3. 3

    Keep 40%

    The dev wallet never sells below 40% of the team bag. Sells go through the public curve, so they move the same price everyone else sees.

① Rule is mechanical, not vibes: threshold, hold window and step size are fixed here before launch. If it is ever changed, the change goes on this page and on @no1dcat.

04 / What this is not

A meme token. No roadmap promises, no treasury, no unlock schedule to monitor, no marketing budget denominated in anything. 500 NOID of backing sits under the supply and is redeemable by burning tokens; everything above that is whatever the market pays.

Supply 500,000 · 60% curve · 40% permanent DEX liquidity on graduation · contract address TBA at the creation block.
