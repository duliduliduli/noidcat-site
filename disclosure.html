2. Buy plan and market making

The token is created at the first block where v2.1 native operations are live, block 279,370, with a creation buy in the same transaction. There is no multi-wallet bundle in the creation block: one wallet, one transaction, one address in the block.

  • Creation buy: 25,000 tokens (5% of supply), paid at curve price, about 90 NOID. This sets the opening price and starts the chart.
  • Team target: 100,000 tokens (20% of supply), reached by accumulation on the public curve over the first hours — not in one block.
  • Hard cap: 22%. Past that, the dev wallet stops buying regardless of price.
  • Market making runs on six addresses from the confirmation block onward. Buy, hold for a short window, sell most of the position back into the curve, repeat. Positions are never sold below their own entry price, so the two-sided trading is not supposed to bleed.
  • Demand following. If the price moves more than 1.5% in a block without a team buy in it, the dev wallet buys within the next block instead of waiting its turn. Real buying is never allowed to outrun the accumulation.

The honest summary: the early volume is largely ours. Six addresses plus the dev wallet are trading a curve nobody else has found yet. What that buys you is a live chart and a price that does not sit flat, not an illusion that a crowd has arrived.

3. Sell ladder

The dev wallet is the only seller, and it sells on a fixed ladder measured against its own average entry price. Nothing is sold below that average.

  • Spacing: at least 120 blocks between rungs, so a single candle cannot trigger two sales.
  • Floor: the dev wallet keeps at least 25% of the bag through graduation and into the DEX pool.
  • Path: every sale goes through the public curve or pool at the same price everyone else sees. No off-market transfers to buyers.
  • Sells are not hidden. The bag sits in a disclosed address; anyone can watch it move.

4. What this is not

  • Not enforced by vesting. The ladder is the declared behaviour of a script, not a contract lock.
  • Not a presale and not an allowlist. There is no allocation to buy and no wallet that gets in early.
  • Not a treasury or a roadmap. There is no marketing wallet denominated in anything, and the team allocation exists to pay for hosting, art and liquidity.
  • Not a claim about price. At launch prices the whole team bag is worth about a hundred dollars. Everything above that is whatever buyers decide.

Backing and supply. Supply is fixed at 500,000 with no mint. Backing is 0.001 NOID per token — 500 NOID in total — deposited at creation, attached permanently to the supply, redeemable by any holder who burns tokens. It is a floor, not a vault. The dev wallet can never withdraw it.
